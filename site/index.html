THE METHOD

Verify before you accuse.

Every tool and model is a sensor. None of them gets to declare truth alone.

01

Map

Entrypoints, identities, assets, provider boundaries, state machines, browser/server splits.

02

Select

Mark hypotheses applicable, not applicable, or unknown. No mindless payload spraying.

03

Hunt

Parallel specialists review authorization, business logic, races, money, web, AI/MCP and supply chain.

04

Verify

A separate reviewer tries to disprove High/Critical candidates and compensating-control assumptions.

05

Fix

Repair the canonical invariant instead of sprinkling symptom patches across the codebase.

06

Prove

Regression, runtime/browser proof, release gate, and an honest residual-risk statement.
